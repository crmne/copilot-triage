Triage keeps answering after your Copilot allowance runs out. The fallback used to place the card and post nothing, so every report filed after the allowance was spent went unanswered.

## New

- **The fallback replies.** Once fewer than 50 premium requests are left and overage is off, triage now runs `gpt-5.6-luna`, the same model as the Copilot engine, through the fallback key (OpenRouter by default), and comments, labels, and closes like a Copilot run. An assessment costs well under a cent. Copilot reviews wait until the allowance returns, since they would bill the same allowance. `fallback-provider` and `fallback-model` still pick another provider, such as OpenAI directly. By @crmne.
- **Previews use the fallback too.** The "Preview real report" workflow can assess a report after the allowance is spent. By @crmne.

## Fixed

- **The fallback no longer crashes.** It failed on every run after the allowance ran out, maintainer comments included, so their cards never moved. By @crmne.
- **A maintainer's comment moves a pull request's card again.** Right after a push to the base branch, GitHub briefly reports mergeability as unknown; triage took the change for a new report and dropped its decision. By @crmne.
- **Pull requests stay where your comment put them.** The sweep no longer moves an approved, mergeable pull request back to Sign off after you asked its author for changes, until the author comments or pushes. By @crmne.

Upgraded to RubyLLM 2.1, with the reply evaluation built on its evaluations. Verified with RuboCop and 360 specs, and with fallback previews of two real Spotifast issues. The live Copilot reply evaluation was not run, because the allowance is spent.
