Withdrawing your review request now works on pull requests Copilot is reviewing too.

## Fixed

- **Review requests leave your queue when they should.** When a pull request moved out of Approve or Review, triage withdrew your review request through GitHub's REST API, which fails whenever a bot such as Copilot is also among the requested reviewers. The job then failed after the board was already updated. Requests and withdrawals now go through GraphQL, and a withdrawal keeps exactly the other pending reviewers, so it never asks Copilot for a new review. By @crmne.
