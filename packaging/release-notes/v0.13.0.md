Your own comments now move your cards, and a pull request no longer looks broken because of triage's own jobs.

## New

- **Your comments move cards.** Triage used to skip a maintainer's comment entirely, so a card stayed put after you asked for a pull request, deferred a request, or took the work. Now your comment runs triage in board-only mode: nothing is posted, and the agent places the card by what you said: Their move when you ask the reporter or author for something, Not now when you defer it, Do when you take it. In an audit of the board, this was the most common reason a card sat in the wrong column. By @crmne.
- **Not now is a move the agent can make,** but only when you have said so. By @crmne.

## Fixed

- **A pull request comes back to you after its author pushes.** Your "changes requested" review kept a contributor's pull request in Their move even after the author addressed it. Once they push a new commit, it returns to Do for your review. By @crmne.
- **Triage's own jobs no longer count as failing checks.** A cancelled triage run made a pull request's checks look red and sent it back to its author. The board now leaves triage's jobs and cancelled or skipped runs out of the checks. By @crmne.
- **Feature pull requests are no longer urgent.** Urgent is for security, data loss, and regressions, never for a feature. By @crmne.

Verified with RuboCop, 317 specs, 23 offline replays, and dry runs on real issues: a maintainer's "Can you please open a PR?" placed the card in Their move three times out of three, and "Calls would be nice, but not now" placed it in Not now.
